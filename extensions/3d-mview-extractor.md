## What it does
Detects 3D model files on the pages you visit and lists them in a popup for download.

## How we use it
- See how other sites build 3D: model size, polygon count, textures.
- Benchmark and learn for our own [Three.js](../stack/threejs.md) scenes.

## Rules
- **Reference and learning only.** Never use a downloaded model in client or public work without the owner's
  permission or a licence.
- It is a small single-developer extension with low ratings and no updates since 2024. Check its permissions before
  installing, and use a separate Chrome profile for it.
- For our own models, compress with glTF-Transform, see [tips/threejs-and-3d-assets.md](../tips/threejs-and-3d-assets.md).
